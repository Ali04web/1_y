Strategies & Clients
01 Pre-Launch: Audit & Intelligence
  • Comprehensive Auditing: Deep-dive into historical data to identify "leaks" in spend and conversion bottlenecks.
  • Market & Consumer Research: Analyzing local trends in diverse global markets to decide the most effective bidding strategies (tCPA vs. tROAS).
  • Landing Page UI/UX Audit: Evaluating page speed and user flow to ensure the high-budget traffic converts into revenue.
02 Structural Excellence: Account & Feed
  • Product Categorization (Zombie vs. Bestsellers): Segmenting the product feed to ensure "Zombie" products don't waste budget while "Bestsellers" get maximum exposure to maintain ROAS.
  • Shopping & Feed Optimization: Writing keyword-rich titles and descriptions to improve organic rank in the Shopping tab.
  • Funnel-Based Campaign Structure:

    Branded: Protecting brand search terms.
    Prospecting: Finding new users via generic and intent-based keywords.
    Competitor: Strategically bidding on rival terms to capture market share.
03 PMax & Creative Mastery
  • Multi-Asset Group Testing: Testing 5 distinct audience signals in PMax: Affinity, In-Market, Custom (Intent), Open (Broad), and Remarketing.
  • Creative Direction: Suggesting trending video and image styles and rotating ad copies regularly to avoid Ad Fatigue.
  • Signal & Theme Integration: Utilizing Search Themes in PMax to provide the AI with better starting points based on consumer research.
04 Execution & Daily Optimization
  • Granular Keyword Hygiene: Daily search term analysis and maintaining high Quality Scores to lower CPCs.
  • Placement Auditing: Reviewing where ads appear (e.g., specific YouTube channels or apps) and excluding non-performing placements.
  • Bulk Management: Using Google Ads Editor for rapid execution across massive accounts.
05 Strategy & Forecasting
  • Internal Target Alignment: Crafting monthly Media Plans and Content Plans that align with client-specific revenue goals and ROI targets.
  • Funnel Strategization: For new clients, starting with high-intent Search and Shopping before expanding into awareness-based Demand Gen.
